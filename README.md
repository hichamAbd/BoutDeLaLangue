# Sur le bout de la langue

A small French notebook for someone who knows French well but has not spoken it for years.
One short challenge a day brings the words back, in everyday spoken French, with an explanation after every answer.

**Open it:** https://hichamabd.github.io/BoutDeLaLangue/

## What is inside

- **Le défi du jour**: 7 questions, about 5 minutes, never timed. An everyday situation (the pharmacy, a phone call,
  the town hall), then conjugation, grammar, vocabulary and an idiom.
- **Jeux**: « Du tac au tac » (quick replies), « Sur le bout de la langue » (find the word from its definition),
  « Trouve la faute », practice by theme, and « À revoir » (missed questions come back at the right time).
- **L'antisèche**: ready-made phrases for when words fail.
- **Mon cahier**: a calendar of finished days, *bons points*, and collectible drawings that each teach an idiom.
- A notebook look by day and a blackboard at night, with a different touch each season.

## Install it

- **Android**: open the link in Chrome, then tap "Install" on the card that appears (or the ⋮ menu, "Add to Home screen").
- **iPhone**: open the link in Safari, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".

It then opens like an app and works without a connection.

## Privacy

Everything stays on the phone. There is no account, no tracking, no advertising, and the app contacts no outside
server. Progress is kept in the browser's storage for this site; Settings (Plus > Réglages) can save it to a file and
restore it. The first name shown on the cover and the birthday are set on the phone itself and are not in this repo.
